Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This app ("Elemental Breaker: Balls") is developed independently. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Data collected

We do not directly collect any personally identifiable data. However, we use third-party services that may collect information for advertising purposes, including: • Advertising ID • Device information (model, operating system) • IP address • Usage data and ad interactions

2. Advertising (Unity Ads)

This app uses Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) to show ads. The service is only activated after you accept ads. Unity Ads may collect and process data to personalize ads according to its privacy policy. For more information about Unity Ads privacy practices, visit: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Google Play Games and purchases

When opened, the app tries to connect you to Google Play Games, a Google service governed by Google's privacy policy. If you use cloud save, your progress (score, gold, stages, balls and upgrades) is stored in your Google Play Games account. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

4. Use of data

Data collected for advertising is used exclusively for: • Showing relevant ads • Measuring ad performance • Preventing fraud

5. Your rights

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, including Brazil's LGPD (Law No. 13,709/2018), you have the right to: • Know what data is collected • Request deletion of your data • Withdraw consent at any time, in Settings > Ad Consent To reset your Advertising ID or turn off personalized ads, open your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads.

6. Data retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Game progress is stored on your device and, if you use cloud save, in your Google Play Games account.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us: Email: j.a.brito@live.com

Cette application utilise Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) pour afficher des annonces. Le service n'est activé qu'après votre acceptation des annonces. À l'ouverture, l'application tente de vous connecter à Google Play Jeux, un service de Google régi par les règles de confidentialité de Google. Nous ne stockons aucune donnée personnelle sur nos propres serveurs. La progression du jeu est stockée sur votre appareil et, si vous utilisez la sauvegarde dans le cloud, dans votre compte Google Play Jeux.
